        How it keeps time

        Every hand is driven by your browser clock and its Intl time-zone data, including daylight-saving rules. Mechanical seconds hands step at their movement's beat rate; quartz hands tick; Spring Drive and smartwatch hands glide.

        What is simulated

        Moon phases follow the mean synodic month. Power reserves, chronograph registers at rest, and the astronomical clock's zodiac ring are illustrative rather than measured.

        About this rendition

        Written from scratch by Claude Opus 5.5 for the World Clock experiment. Brand and character names identify the objects depicted and imply no affiliation. Back to all renditions.
