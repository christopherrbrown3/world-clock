# GPT 6 Luna reference notes

GPT 6 Luna is a standalone rendition authored from the shared 62-object catalog. Its page has its own layout, time-zone code, city state, and SVG drawings; it does not borrow another model page. The watch and clock images are drawn in inline SVG, and the local photographs are not shipped with the project.

## Local photo references

The local reference archive contains 323 files across 35 populated face folders. The photos directly consulted for this rendition included:

- Rolex Submariner Date 126610LN: black dial, ceramic bezel, Mercedes handset, bracelet, and date magnifier.
- Omega Speedmaster Moonwatch: stepped dial, tachymeter, pump pushers, and three-register placement.
- Omega Silver Snoopy Award 50th Anniversary: silver and blue dial treatment and six-o’clock register.
- Prague Orloj: layered astronomical dial and restored gilt and blue structure.
- Grand Seiko Snowflake SBGA211: pale textured dial, titanium case, and blue Spring Drive seconds.
- Richard Mille RM 011: tonneau silhouette and layered chronograph layout.
- Ingersoll Mickey Mouse: character-hand construction and period dial proportions.

The archive is incomplete relative to the catalog and contains variant differences. For example, Royal Oak photos include older black-dial 15400/15500 variants and a chronograph, while the catalog entry is the blue 15510ST. Those photos were treated as references for case and bracelet construction only; the Luna drawing follows the catalog’s blue three-hand model. The Explorer folder also contains Explorer II and other variants, so the drawing follows the catalog’s 36 mm 124270. The same catalog-first rule applies wherever a local image differs from the named object.

## Rendering and behavior

Each of the 62 faces has an individual key, name, material palette, dial treatment, case or housing shape, markers, hands, strap or bracelet, and complication layout. Distinct render paths cover digital watches, the Apple Watch, the Lange 1, the Orloj, architectural clocks, a pocket watch, an alarm clock, and character watches. Mechanical complications that cannot be derived from browser time remain visual simulations; the chronograph tool is an interactive elapsed-time display.

City clocks use the browser’s IANA time-zone data. The interface saves a city’s selected face locally, supports city search and removal, shuffles one or all faces, sorts cities by current UTC offset, and includes a searchable 62-piece library and inspection view.

## Limits

SVG geometry simplifies fine engraving, surface finishing, and small production details. The power-reserve indicators and calendar or moon displays are not connected to real movements; the moon phase is a browser-time calculation. The Snoopy figure is a small vector interpretation, not a reproduced dial illustration.

The source and project metadata were reviewed directly. A rendered screenshot could not be captured because the available browser rejected the local page under its URL security policy, so the final appearance has not received browser-based visual verification.
