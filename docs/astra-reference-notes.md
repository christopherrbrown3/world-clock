# Astra reference notes

[Astra](../versions/astra.html) is an independent rendition of the full [62-piece catalog](watch-face-catalog.md). Its interface, time engine, SVG toolkit, and watch drawings were authored from scratch. Earlier model pages and the active working build are preserved.

Supplied photographs were inspected for case shape, proportions, dial print, markers, hand geometry, apertures and material finish. Manufacturer, museum and dealer images supplement missing references and identify catalog-specific variants. Photographs are research material only; the shipped page contains SVG geometry and no raster watch faces or external dependencies.

The drawings approximate reflected light, brushing, engraving and dial textures with vector geometry. Very small lettering and brand artwork are simplified. The watches show the selected city's time and calendar through the browser's IANA time-zone data. GMT hands show UTC. Chronograph controls measure elapsed time in this browser; power-reserve and complex astronomical mechanisms are visual studies, not instrument simulations. Historical objects whose exact reference could not be verified are identified below.

## Intentional approximations

Sapphire reflections, brushed metal, leather, sunray surfaces and gem facets are analytical SVG approximations rather than optical simulations. Small logos and the Snoopy/Secret Service artwork are redrawn silhouettes and linework. Microprinting and register subdivisions are simplified where necessary to remain coherent at card size. Chronograph displays use the application's elapsed-time controls; they do not claim to simulate movement mechanics.

## Renderer validation

- All 18 draw functions execute against the shared toolkit with no undefined or NaN output.
- An isolated Chromium gallery rendered all 18 with no runtime errors and no duplicate SVG IDs.
- A full gallery capture was visually inspected at 10:10 with seconds and GMT hands away from twelve.
- Main and register hands use explicit pivots; all date apertures have unique clip paths.
- Parent integration owns live time, timezone/date boundary checks, chronograph controls and full interaction/mobile QA.

## Binding details

`regatta` is a 10-minute reverse countdown across the Yacht-Master II's 300-degree scale. `hour24` is the Jorg Gray right-hand 24-hour time register. The Type XX right elapsed-minute register requests a 900-second revolution; the Zenith lower and Jorg Gray left registers request 3600 seconds. Zenith's main chronograph uses a 10-second revolution while its right register uses 60 seconds. Every ordinary chronograph is at rest until the application's elapsed-time control starts it.
