## Motion and approximations

The city list uses IANA time zones and sorts by current UTC offset. Live inscriptions follow each dial’s local date. Gallery timepieces use the selected city’s time. Inspection can change the time zone, pose the local hands at 10:10, or operate the counters and regatta countdown. Moon displays use a mean lunar cycle. Power reserves demonstrate indicator travel with simulated readings, and the Apple Watch rings show example progress.
